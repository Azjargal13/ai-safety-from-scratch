## Frontier Labs (with dedicated safety efforts)

**United States / West**

- **Anthropic** — Constitutional AI, interpretability, Responsible Scaling Policy
- **OpenAI** — Alignment & Preparedness teams
- **Google DeepMind** — Frontier Safety Framework, scalable oversight
- **Safe Superintelligence (SSI)** — Ilya Sutskever’s lab (safety-focused)
- **xAI** — Has safety work (generally rated lower externally)
- **Meta AI** — Open-source safety tools (LlamaGuard etc.)

**China**

- **ByteDance (Seed)** — Created a dedicated Alignment & Preparedness team in 2026 (one of the first clear standalone safety teams among Chinese frontier labs)
- **Alibaba (Qwen)**, **Tencent**, **Baidu**, **DeepSeek**, **Moonshot**, **Zhipu (Z.ai)**, **MiniMax**, **StepFun** — All do some safety-related work; public detailed safety reporting is still limited compared to leading Western labs
- **Shanghai AI Lab (SAIL)** — Important state-backed lab; publishes Frontier AI Risk Management Framework and runs evaluations

## China – Key Academic & Safety Hubs

- **Beijing AI Safety Hub (BASH / Qingzhi AI Safety Hub)** — Tsinghua University  
  Co-directed by **Sören Mindermann**, Prof. Andrew Yao, and Prof. Xu Wei.  
  New hub for Chinese and international AI safety researchers. Runs residencies. Inspired by places like Constellation.

- **Tsinghua University (College of AI)** — Hosts BASH and is becoming a major center for frontier AI safety research in China. Sören Mindermann joined as Assistant Professor in 2026.

- **Shanghai AI Lab (SAIL)** — Strong output on risk frameworks and evaluations.

- **Beijing Institute of AI Safety and Governance (Beijing-AISI)**

- **CAICT** (China Academy of Information and Communications Technology) — Runs official-style AI Safety Benchmarks.

- Research groups at **Peking University**, **Fudan University**, and other top universities.

## Notes

- The Chinese ecosystem has grown significantly in research output (especially agent safety), but public detailed safety evaluations and transparency still lag behind the leading Western labs.
- BASH (Beijing AI Safety Hub) is one of the most notable new developments in 2026 for bridging Chinese and international AI safety research.
- Many people move between frontier labs, independent orgs, academia, and government institutes.
- New organizations and fellowships appear frequently — this list is not exhaustive.
