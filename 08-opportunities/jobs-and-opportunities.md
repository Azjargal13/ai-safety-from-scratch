# Opportunities & Jobs in AI Safety

Useful links for finding roles, fellowships, and opportunities in AI safety and related fields.

---

## Best Starting Points

- **[80,000 Hours](https://80000hours.org/)**  
  Excellent career advice and job board. Strong coverage of AI safety, governance, and related high-impact paths.

- **[AISafety.com Jobs](https://aisafety.com/)**  
  Regularly updated list of jobs, fellowships, and opportunities in the AI safety ecosystem.

- **[Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/)** & **[LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/)**  
  Many organizations and researchers post openings or calls for collaborators here.

## Fellowships & Research Programs

(These are often good entry points)

- MATS (ML Alignment & Theory Scholars)
- Anthropic Fellows Program
- PIBBSS Fellowship
- ERA Fellowship
- Singapore AI Safety Fellowship
- Beijing AI Safety Residency (BASH / Tsinghua)
- BlueDot Impact courses (good preparation)

Check [AISafety.com/training](https://aisafety.com/training) for current deadlines and programs.

## Other Useful Resources

- **[80,000 Hours AI Safety Career Guide](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/artificial-intelligence/)**  
  In-depth advice on paths into the field.

- Organization mailing lists and Twitter/X accounts of major labs and independent orgs (many roles are announced there first).

- Academic opportunities: look at groups led by researchers working on alignment, interpretability, evaluations, and governance at universities.
