# Key Papers & Reading List

A curated list of important papers and resources, roughly ordered from more accessible to more advanced.

---

## Beginner-Friendly Starting Points

- **Concrete Problems in AI Safety** (Amodei et al., 2016)  
  Classic paper that framed many technical safety problems in ML terms.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06565>

- **AI Safety via Debate** (Irving, Christiano, Amodei, 2018)  
  Early influential paper on using debate for scalable oversight.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.00899>

- **Alignment for Advanced Machine Learning Systems** (various summaries)  
  Good conceptual overviews can be found on the Alignment Forum and AISafety.info.

---

## Foundational Technical Papers

- **Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems** (Hubinger et al., 2019)  
  Introduced mesa-optimization and deceptive alignment. Extremely influential.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01820>

- **Goal Misgeneralization in Deep Reinforcement Learning** (Langosco et al., 2022)  
  Clear experimental demonstration of goal misgeneralization.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14111>

- **Deep Reinforcement Learning from Human Preferences** (Christiano et al., 2017)  
  Early foundation for RLHF.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03741>

- **Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback** (Ouyang et al., 2022) — InstructGPT paper  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155>

- **Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback** (Bai et al., 2022)  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073>

- **Direct Preference Optimization** (Rafailov et al., 2023)  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290>

---

## Important Later / Empirical Work

- **Weak-to-Strong Generalization** (Burns et al., OpenAI, 2023)  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09390>

- Papers and reports from **Anthropic**, **Redwood Research**, **Apollo Research**, and **METR** on:
  - Alignment faking
  - Scheming / deceptive behavior
  - Control protocols
  - Dangerous capability evaluations

(These are published regularly — check the organizations’ websites and the Alignment Forum.)

---

## Major Reports (Highly Recommended)

- **International AI Safety Report** (led by Yoshua Bengio)  
  Important international scientific consensus reports (2025 and 2026 editions).

- Lab policy documents:
  - Anthropic’s Responsible Scaling Policy
  - OpenAI’s Preparedness Framework
  - Google DeepMind’s Frontier Safety Framework

## Good Places to Read Ongoing Discussion

- [Alignment Forum](https://www.alignmentforum.org/)
- [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/) (especially AI Alignment section)
- [AISafety.info](https://aisafety.info/)
- Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, Redwood, Apollo, and METR blogs / research pages

---

## Suggested Reading Order for Beginners

1. Concrete Problems in AI Safety (2016)
2. Short explanations on AISafety.info
3. Risks from Learned Optimization (at least the core ideas)
4. Goal Misgeneralization paper
5. InstructGPT + Constitutional AI papers
6. Recent evals and control work from METR, Apollo, and Redwood
7. International AI Safety Report (latest version)
